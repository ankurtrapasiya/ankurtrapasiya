### What I work on

- **Performance.** Latency and throughput in JVM services and streaming consumers. I measure first, change one thing, measure again, and publish the numbers with the method.
- **Correctness under load.** Idempotency, exactly-once-ish delivery, backpressure, and the failure modes of systems that cannot be wrong.
- **Mechanism over settings.** I care more about *why* a system behaves a certain way (the lock, the fsync, the partition) than which flag to flip.
